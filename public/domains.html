Your domain, your invitation.

Everly has the database layer ready for custom domains. The final activation requires DNS and the hosting provider that serves the invitation site.

1

Choose a domain

Example: jasmineandomar.com

2

Add the domain in Everly

The account system creates a secure pending domain record and verification token.

3

Configure DNS

Point the domain to the final Everly hosting endpoint using the DNS record required by that provider.

4

Verify and activate

After DNS propagation, the domain can move from pending to verified/active.

Important

No fake activation

Everly will not display a domain as active until DNS/hosting verification is actually implemented. This prevents broken links and misleading customers.
